Effect of the Kullback–Leibler Prior on Latent Space Structure in Breast Ultrasound Representation Learning

Developed a Variational Autoencoder framework to investigate how KL regularization shapes latent space organization in breast ultrasound images. The study evaluates reconstruction quality, latent separability, clustering behavior, and downstream diagnostic utility for medical imaging.

  • 780+ breast ultrasound images analyzed
  • PyTorch-based Variational Autoencoder
  • Latent space analysis using UMAP and t-SNE
  • Cluster metrics, centroid distances, and latent traversal evaluation
