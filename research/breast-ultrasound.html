Accepted Research • IntelliSys 2026 • Amsterdam

Deep Generative Modeling for Breast Ultrasound Representation Learning

A PyTorch-based Variational Autoencoder study analyzing how KL regularization shapes latent space structure, reconstruction quality, and medical image representation learning.

Research Overview

Effect of the Kullback–Leibler Prior on Latent Space Structure

This research investigates how Variational Autoencoders learn compact, structured representations from breast ultrasound images and how those representations support downstream analysis.

Problem

Breast ultrasound images contain high visual variability and subtle tissue patterns, making representation learning important for medical AI systems.

Approach

A Variational Autoencoder was trained to reconstruct images while learning a structured latent space controlled by KL regularization.

Evaluation

The latent space was analyzed using reconstruction quality, UMAP, t-SNE, cluster metrics, centroid distances, and latent traversal.

Outcome

The work was accepted at IntelliSys 2026 in Amsterdam, Netherlands.
