Reliable Clinical Decision Support using Retrieval-Augmented Generation

A research framework evaluating confidence calibration, evidence utilization, JSON reliability, and failure patterns in clinical question answering systems.

Project Overview

Evaluating reliability beyond accuracy.

Datasets

PubMedQA, MedQA, and MMLU-Med were used for cross-dataset clinical QA evaluation.

Pipeline

A fixed retrieval pipeline supports generation-side reliability analysis.

Models

Qwen and Mistral were evaluated for clinical answer generation behavior.

Analysis

Confidence calibration, evidence use, invalid JSON, and failure taxonomy were analyzed.

System Workflow

From clinical question to reliability analysis.

Clinical Question
↓
Fixed Retrieval
↓
LLM Generation
↓
Reliability Metrics
↓
Failure Analysis

Why it matters

Clinical AI needs reliability, not only accuracy.

A model can produce a correct-looking answer while being overconfident, poorly calibrated, unsupported by evidence, or inconsistent across datasets.

Research Contributions

Reliability dimensions studied in the pipeline.

Confidence Calibration

Analyzed whether confidence aligns with correctness across datasets.

Evidence Utilization

Studied how retrieved evidence is used or ignored during answer generation.

Failure Taxonomy

Categorized errors into interpretable failure patterns for clinical QA reliability.
